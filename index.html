What We Need Most

We can buy staples in bulk cheaply, so cash goes further than cans. But if you would rather bring food, these four items run out first every single week:

  • Peanut butter
  • Canned chicken and tuna
  • Diapers, sizes 4 and 5
  • Laundry detergent

Volunteer With Us

Questions People Ask

Do I have to prove I need help?

No. We do not ask for income verification, ID, or a referral. Take what your household needs.

How often can I come?

Once a week. The monthly diaper distribution is separate and does not count against that.

Can I donate food that is past the "best by" date?

Shelf-stable items are usually fine past that date, but we cannot accept anything opened, home-canned, or refrigerated. When in doubt, call first.

Is this only for church members?

No. The pantry serves all of Chester County. You will never be asked where you worship.
